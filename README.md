I am an Animal Breeding, Genetics, and Systems Immunology researcher. My work targets the critical intersection of genetic susceptibility, livestock immunogenetics, and host-microbiome interactions. I leverage transcriptomics, metagenomics, and structural proteomics to engineer host-centric alternatives to conventional antimicrobials and mitigate disease vulnerabilities in tropical poultry production systems.

---

### 🔬 Featured Research Projects

#### 🧬 1. Immunomodulation & Host Immunity Kinetics
* **Focus:** Investigating alternative therapeutic targets (*Mantis religiosa* egg) to optimize cytokine balances and enhance humoral immunity under sheep red blood cells challenge.
* **Core Tasks:** Growth and Adaptive traits analysis. Modeled hemagglutination titer response and verified homeostatic balance between pro- and anti-inflammatory genes.
* **Stack:** `R`, `SAS`, `RT-qPCR`
* 💻 [**View Repository**](https://github.com)

#### 🦠 2. Host-Microbiome-Phenotype Axis
* **Focus:** Taxonomic profiling and differential abundance workflows across distinct *FUNAAB Alpha* broiler chicken genotypes (e.g., Frizzle-feathered lines).
* **Core Tasks:** Performed taxonomic profiling (phylum-to-genus) and differential abundance analysis, successfully identifying key microbial biomarkers across FUNAAB Alpha chicken genotypes. Isolated short-chain fatty acid (SCFA)-producing biomarkers (*Faecalibacterium*, *Fusicatenibacter*) linked to performance and mapped potential host genetic-microbiome interactions.
* **Stack:** `R Programming`, `STAMP`, `16S rRNA Metagenomics`
* 💻 [**View Repository**](https://github.com)

#### 📊 3. High-Throughput Functional Transcriptomics
* **Focus:** Transcriptomic re-analysis of Avian Pathogenic *Escherichia coli* (APEC) infected broiler liver tissue (Dataset: GSE282645).
* **Core Tasks:** Uncovered large-scale epigenetic pathway reprogramming (chromatin organization/DNA methylation) explaining 71.8% of transcriptional variance on PC1.
* **Stack:** `DESeq2`, `ggplot2`, `Galaxy Africa Platform`, `RNA-Seq Pipeline`
* 💻 [**View Repository**](https://github.com)

#### 🔭 4. Proposed Doctoral Research
* **Focus:** Understanding the cecal transcriptome and localized mucosal immunity networks of *Eimeria tenella*-challenged chickens administered *Mantis religiosa* egg as a functional dietary additive.
